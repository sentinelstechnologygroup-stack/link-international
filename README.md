# Link International Trading Company

Clean marketing website for Link International Trading Company.

## Business Positioning

Link International Trading Company is a sourcing and trade facilitation company that helps buyers and sellers connect across international markets.

LITC supports product sourcing, supplier discovery, buyer introductions, procurement coordination, documentation support, and shipping coordination through appropriate third-party providers.

LITC does not act as the buyer, seller, manufacturer, shipper, carrier, freight forwarder, customs broker, importer of record, or exporter of record unless expressly agreed in writing.

npm install

## Compliance Note

Link International Trading Company provides sourcing support, buyer-seller connection support, trade coordination, and commercial facilitation services. LITC is not the buyer, seller, manufacturer, shipper, carrier, freight forwarder, customs broker, importer of record, or exporter of record unless expressly agreed in writing. Shipping, customs, insurance, legal, tax, and regulatory services may require qualified third-party providers.
